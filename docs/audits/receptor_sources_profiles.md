## 8. Caveats

* Developmental stage: pupal (24-96 h APF). Receptor genes are still being switched on through 60-96 h APF (Kurmangaliyev 2020's central finding is the late, coordinated pan-neuronal synaptic program); the 96 h rows are pharate adults, ~1 day before the 1-3 day adults of Özel 2021 and the 5-day-plus heads of Davis 2020. Treat 96 h profiles as adult proxies, not adult measurements.
* Sex: female (roX1 / roX2 essentially absent) (section 3); MaleCNS is male.
* Cluster purity and the authors' annotation basis are not verifiable here (article paywalled); the T4c / T4d swap in the original release was corrected by the authors (README 11/02/20) and the corrected metadata is used.
* Small 96 h clusters (Dm4, Dm1, LC10b, LPC1, LPLC1, Pm3, Pm4 ...) have < 30 cells at that timepoint; use the `late` rows for them.
* No author-provided per-cluster table: the per-cluster numbers are ours (one streaming pass, `stream_counts`), cached per cell in `data/external/kurmangaliyev2020/_percell_54genes.npz` (keyed on the matrix SHA-256).
