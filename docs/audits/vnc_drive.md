## 1. Question and design

`deficit_turning.md` 6.3 and `deficit_rotation.md` 3(3) localised one missing afferent: every `vnc_sensory` /
`sensory_ascending` proprioceptor sits at 0 Hz in the room, so the walking VNC is open-loop (IN12B014 / IN19A003 at
12-15 Hz inhibit DNa02 with no proprioceptive input), the ascending cells that would carry a turn report never fire
(AN04B003 <- SNppxx / SNpp39, AN07B035 <- SNpp45, AN07B037_a / AN06A026 <- the haltere SApp) and PS196_b -- the one
efference-copy input of GLNO -- stays at 0.03-0.16 Hz; DNa02 makes 0 synapses onto the PS196_b / LAL / GLNO chain, so the
efferent rotation arm of `deficit_rotation.md` could not excite it. `interp_atlas.md` 3 showed the wiring works when
driven (`superclass.vnc_sensory` rank-1 mover of the haltere and power readouts, `class.mechanosensory_proprioceptive`
rank 1 for `steer_LR`). The transducer (`senses.Proprioception`) drives those afferents from the body state the VNC motor
neurons produce (leg-MN rate per side, haltere-MN rate, ground contact) -- a body-state -> afferent-rate model of the
`Wind` -> JO kind, which the project rule names as the legitimate mechanism for a wired-but-never-driven population --
and its smoke (`proprioception_transducer.md` 5: 4 flies x 5 s, fenced) showed the injection is what the laws say,
AN04B003 and PS196_b fire, DNa02 does not move. This task asks the three questions that smoke could not:

1. do the afferents reach the ascending cells and beyond, per stage, in the plain fly's 60 s room protocol;
2. does the plain fly's steering change (yaw-rate SD, DNa02 L-R, leg L-R), with the run scatter and `common.compare`;
3. does the compass loop see a self-turn once PS196_b fires (the efferent rotation arm, repeated with the sense on).

**Arms** (`probe_vnc_drive.py` `ARMS`; every spec is `Proprioception.parse_spec`'s grammar):

| arm | spec | what it tests | classification |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | none (the shipped path) | the control; every difference is against A | -- |
| B | `all` | the four channels with the default laws (chordotonal 10-150 Hz on the leg-MN rate, hair plate 5-100, campaniform 50 Hz on the ground, haltere = haltere-MN rate) | mechanism the connectome implies (a transducer on an existing, wired, never-driven afferent population), with the documented body-model limitation that the leg variable is the MN rate, not a joint angle |
| C | `chordotonal,hair_plate,campaniform` | the leg channels alone (the AN04B003 / AN07B035 / IN19A003 / DNa02-direct routes) | the same |

| E | `all+haltere_coriolis` | B plus the labelled STOP-GAP term `(1 + g|yaw_rate|)` on the haltere channel: `body.Locomotion`'s realised yaw scalar fed back as if it were a sense | **stop-gap control arm** (a loop through a hand-written readout, never a default); it is a *positive control of the pathway*: a body-locked rate change at the afferents that, being `|yaw|` on a group `MotorRates` does not side-split, is unsigned and bilateral by construction |

Three readings. (i) The leg channels reach DNa02 in two steps through AN04B003 (SNppxx + SNpp39, 702 = 386 + 316 of

AN07B037_a. (iii) GLNO has no afferent input within two steps of any weight (11.9 mV^2 at best); everything from the
body reaches it through PS196_b (16.7 mV per PS196_b volley, 1,801 synapses = 19.2 % of GLNO's raw input) or through

`proprioception_transducer.md` 7.2), so the only sided sources on the table are the leg channels through
AN04B003 -> PS047_b -> PS196_b (k3, +1.6e4 / +1.3e4).

## 8. Classification, and what a default adoption would require

| item | classification | evidence here |
|---|---|---|
| chordotonal / hair-plate channels (leg-MN rate -> Hz within the literature bracket) | **mechanism the connectome implies**, body-model limitation documented (MN rate, not a joint angle; no stance phase) | they drive the AN04B003 -> DNa02 and AN04B003 -> PS047_b -> PS196_b routes, relax the VNC's open-loop inhibitors, double the leg L-R and add 0.47-0.69 deg/s of yaw-rate SD (sections 4-5) |
| campaniform (ground load) | mechanism the connectome implies | 12 cells; on in every ground frame; not separable from the leg arm here (arm C bundles it) |
| haltere (= haltere-MN rate) | mechanism the connectome implies; symmetric by construction | alone it raises PS196_b to 0.657 / 0.977 Hz and GLNO to 0.224 / 0.211 Hz through CB0675 / GNG580 and adds -12 / -13 mV/s to DNa02 through PS059 (arm D); steering unchanged |
| haltere Coriolis term (arm E) | **stop-gap, labelled control arm, never a default** -- a loop through `body.Locomotion`'s yaw scalar | on the ground indistinguishable from B (a 3.6 % term); in a self-turn a positive but **stable** loop through the haltere MNs (command 9.38 -> 80.10 Hz, MN 9.38 -> 27.55 Hz, realised gain ~0.26) that still gives PS196_b no sign |
| every number in the laws | transducer constants with literature brackets, exposed as parameters | none set against behaviour; no ceiling was reached (room maxima 23.5 / 14.2 / 50.0 / 11.3 Hz against 150 / 100 / 100 / 250; the highest command anywhere is E's **80.1 Hz** haltere in the compass turn -- higher than the smoke's, still a third of the 250 Hz ceiling) |

**Nothing is recommended as a default.** What an adoption of arm B as the shipped path would require, in order:

1. *The suite.* Section 7: `rest.spikes_per_step` reads **6.0 against `< 5`** with the sense on, because the check is
   defined as "no input" and the transducer's tonic terms (10 / 5 / 50 Hz on 740 afferents) are input. The other nine
   checks all pass their bounds, but four of them move by 37-81 % under B (`taste.MN9_hz` -62 %, `smell.PN_hz` -64 %,
   `smell.KC_active` -37 %, `walk.GF_max_hz` +81 %) -- the adoption cost is broader than a `rest` redefinition. A
   default would need either a redefinition of `rest` (a fly standing still still has proprioceptors firing -- the
   check as written measures the model with its senses cut) or a ledger row that says what a resting VNC should read,
   plus scored brackets for the four moved measures, the full suite (`--sections all`) and `hops` (2,400 fly-s x >= 3
   draws: the power readout drops, the haltere-MN rate rises, and `receptor_integration.md` G.5's take-off rate is the
   check that would see either).
2. *The ledger.* The six `lit.*` rows are `op report` brackets, not scores; a default needs at least one measured
   Drosophila rate per channel (the first FeCO / hair-plate / campaniform electrophysiology in Hz), the ascending
   walking-state row turned into a scored one (AN04B003 3.7 Hz walking vs 0.3-0.6 resting is now a measurable model
   statement), and the corrections of section 9 applied.
3. *The loop caveat.* The transducer reads the previous frame's motor readout: VNC -> MN rate -> afferent -> VNC. It
   passes through no hand-written scalar, but it is a loop with gain (the haltere afferents raise the haltere MNs
   they read: +2.2 Hz here). **This precondition is DONE (2026-09-13):** `BatchSim.state_dict` carries the motor
   snapshot, `load_state_dict` restores it, a partial `reset(rows)` zeroes only the selected rows, and
   `tests/test_proprioception.py::CheckpointAndResetTests` covers the resumed run's CPU bit-identity and the partial
   reset (section 9.6). What a default still needs is a
   statement of the loop gain per channel at the operating point (the haltere channel's is the one to measure:
   `d haltere_MN / d haltere_afferent_Hz` from arm D vs A is +1.659 Hz per +10.205 Hz, i.e. **0.163** -- stable, but
   not zero; the E arm's in-turn realisation of the same gain is ~0.26, which is what the stop-gap arm actually
   demonstrates: a loop that converges to a 27.6 Hz MN / 80 Hz afferent fixed point, not a loop above 1).
4. *What it would not fix.* Turning (DNa02 at -1.17 / -1.71 mV under B) and the compass (PS196_b symmetric) stay where
   the two deficit audits put them; the data-driven items that remain are the ones they named -- the LLPC1 / LPT22
   trace, the JO calibration, a leg cycle in the body (which would make the leg channels sided during a turn and is
   a `body.py` mechanism, not a transducer parameter), and a side-split haltere MN readout in `motor.py`.
