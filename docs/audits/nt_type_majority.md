## 6. Decision

**Not adopted (round 4).** The rule at the round-2 standard proposes three entries (JO-unclear, MeTu1, T2a -> acetylcholine; 36 cells) and every one of those cells has zero output synapses in the raw table: the scratch cache `out/cache_605ab2bd` is byte-identical in W to the shipped cache and the default receptor model's fast signs are identical (section 5), so the entries cannot change any simulation. The three candidate suite runs (`out/r4_ntmaj_{1,2,3}.json`, the fixed round-4 benchmark, B200) score 26 PASS / 1 FAIL / 2 KNOWN GAP; against the only default baseline on record when they were run, `out/r3_default_{1,2,3}.json` (27/0/2; `out/r4_default_*.json` did not exist yet), `walk.power_max_hz` moves PASS -> FAIL (46.10 half-applied vs 79.47 / 79.47 / 80.09 fully applied) and the legacy `loom.GF_peak_hz` rises 27-32 -> 46.9-54.3 Hz -- both are the benchmark fix of round-4 item 1 (the walk / motion sections now build the optic lobe with the receptor lookup), not the entries, by the W identity; the other values that move are the same half-applied -> fully-applied sections (`walk.GF_max_hz` 8.52 -> 4.61, `walk.power_sustained_hz` 21.36 -> 37.9-39.9, `motion.min_dsi` 0.17 -> 0.23, all PASS), and every room section (taste, smell, dn, loom_escape, walk_gf, rotation, object, bitter, wind, odour, compass) is identical or within the baseline's own scatter. So the literal criterion is not met against the available baseline, and would be met trivially against a fixed-benchmark default; either way there is nothing to adopt: a label-only change to 36 silent cells does not justify rebuilding the local and shared caches. The entries and their evidence are listed above (section 1) for the record and can be added to `TYPE_NT_OVERRIDE` at any later rebuild without a suite run.

What the rule settles: (i) the type majority cannot replace `UNKNOWN_NT_OVERRIDE_REGEX` -- of the 27 AL LN cells the regex relabels (13 types, 136,223 raw output synapses), 0 are covered by the rule at any threshold, 5 of the 13 types have no labelled member, and in the 8 that do the labelled siblings (1-2 cells each) say GABA in 2 types, acetylcholine in 3 and glutamate in 3 (section 3); (ii) the round-1 skeptic's 496 presynaptic cells / 221,605 synapses (reproduced exactly on `out/cache_pre_override`; `out/r4_type_majority_pre_override.md`) are 443 cells / 189,926 synapses after the round-2 override, sitting in 99 types of which 95 (406 cells, 188,608 synapses) have no transcriptome call and no Nern prediction, so no threshold reaches them under a two-source standard; the majority rule alone at the round-2 thresholds (>= 4 labelled, share >= 0.8) reaches 6 types / 58 cells / 8,275 synapses (0.007 % of all), and even at (>= 1 labelled, share >= 0.5) 432 cells / 189,459 synapses -- labels from a single sibling cell, which is the FlyWire / MaleCNS per-cell classifier's own unclear call restated. The unknown-NT population is dominated by types with no labelled member at all (864 typed cells, 582,367 synapses, e.g. INXXX045 31,122, AVLP538 27,352, PVLP106 21,957, DNd02 21,497, GLNO 17,698) and by 1,054 untyped cells (51,918 synapses): a per-cell rule (the T-bar argmax leaning in `nt_audit.md` section 1d) is the only route left to them.
