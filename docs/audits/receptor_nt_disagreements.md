# Transmitter cross-check: transcriptome vs MaleCNS consensus vs Nern 2025

Generated by `scripts/build_receptor_table.py` from `flyverse/data/nt_by_type_transcriptome.csv` (483 MaleCNS types with an expression profile) and `type_map_nern2025.csv`. `model sign` = `NT_SIGN` of the model's label (mode of `neurons.nt` over the type's cells, after the AL-LN override); `synapses` = raw (uncapped) output synapses of the type on the model's node set. Rule of the plan (step 3): a sign / label change is proposed only where two of the three sources agree against the model, i.e. the transcriptome call and the Nern 2025 prediction agree and differ from the MaleCNS consensus. The transcriptome call rule and its thresholds are in `docs/audits/receptor_rules.md`.
